Photo Gallery Folder
===================

This folder is ready for you to upload your family photos for the baptism service webpage.

Instructions:
1. Upload your photos to this folder
2. Recommended image formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP
3. Recommended size: 400x300 pixels or larger
4. The photos will automatically display in a responsive grid layout

Current placeholder photos will be replaced with your uploaded images.
